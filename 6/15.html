A close up image of the strange tower fills your mind. It is futuristic yet decayed, a strange mixture of engineering achievement, technological innovation and neglect. Beyond is a plane of bluish slate, dotted with grey domes. Near it lies a great dust-filled crater, at the centre, its base standing quite free of the dust, is a shiny black monolith. Suddenly the mist begins to recede at a great rate. Either it is bored by your questioning or something has frightened it. Turn to 60.
