A strange impression of peaceful serenity floods your mind as the incandescent blue gas cocoons you and then you shrink. Your size dwindles rapidly - and the ground surges up towards you until you are no larger than an ant perched between two grains of sand as large as small boulders. Uncannily the transformation barely perturbs you. The thought of scrambling across the sea of sand grain boulders seems too much trouble to contemplate. You settle down to rest. Turn to 31.
