Muscles aching from the effort you force your way up the sheer rock towards the lip of the crevasse. Two yards from the top you pause to search for the next hand hold as your pursuers dismount. Your foot slips a little and you grimly hang on for your life as the shadow of a kneeling Knight falls on you. You look up in time to see the falling sword blade which severs your arm. This time your fall, and your body, are broken by unyielding granite. You have lived into the twilight of the universe but not long enough to tell the tale.
