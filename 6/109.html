You step lightheartedly towards the purple haze, but as soon as you enter it, you pass into an altogether less heartwarming environment. You are now cocooned in a chill blue glow. A strange impression of peaceful serenity floods your mind and then you shrink. Your size dwindles rapidly - and the ground surges up towards you until you are no larger than an ant perched between two grains of sand as large as small boulders. Uncannily the transformation barely perturbs you. The thought of scrambling across the sea of sand grain boulders seems too much trouble to contemplate.

You settle down to rest. Turn to 31
