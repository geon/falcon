You wait for a few seconds, sweating with fear, as the tank is filled with an awful whining sound. Then the bullet slams into the commander's chest and he collapses. Whilst the driver covers the other two crewmen, you remove the blindfold. Quickly you tie them up and then order the driver to turn the tank around and drive away at full speed. Moments later, the radio crackles into life, 'Otto, this is Meyer - what are you doing? Stop, I order you!' Poking your head out of the hatch you can see the other Tiger tanks in pursuit. One of them fires - a geyser of dust erupts with a dull crump nearby. Your tank begins to climb a ridge. You reach the top when there is an explosion and a flash of flames. The tank shudders and slews to a stop, one of its tracks blown off. You leap out of the turret and scramble down the ridge. Turn to 396.
