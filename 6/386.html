Fromm smiles expansively and says, 'Excellent, excellent, I'm so glad you're being sensible, so glad. Meyer, place the timefarer in solitary confinement under close guard.' Turning to you he continues, 'It is for your own safety, of course. The last time the Dragonmen came and plucked Bloodhound from our midst, leaving a trail of death and destruction as they did so, I might add. It will take a few hours to ready my men - once that is done, you will take us to your Time Machine and take us home. Goodbye for now, Falcon.' You are taken to another wooden cottage, a square hut with a single door. It is spartanly furnished inside with a wooden bed, table and chairs - no windows. An electric bulb gives light. The guards push you in and lock the door. You are alone. Turn to 416.
