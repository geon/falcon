You hurry quickly into the embrace of the green flame-like mists and, before you know where you are, you are cocooned in a chill blue glow, where the blue and yellow mist meets the green glow is produced, you are now beyond it, cocooned in blue. A strange impression of peaceful serenity floods your mind and then you shrink. Your size dwindles rapidly - and the ground surges up towards you until you are no larger than an ant perched between two grains of sand as large as small boulders. Uncannily the transformation barely perturbs you. The thought of scrambling across the sea of sand grain boulders seems too much trouble to contemplate.
