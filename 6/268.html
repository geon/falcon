The others stiffen in surprise, and you remove your blindfold. The gunner says, 'Otto, listen - its mind trickery. Fight back!' 'Shut up,' you say, 'or I'll have him shoot you.' You tell them to drop their weapons and then tie up the gunner and radio man. You force the commander to tell the driver to turn around as fast as possible. They seem familiar with mind control - Bloodhound must have given them a hard time. You ask one of them about Bloodhound, and he says, maliciously, 'If you're looking for your friend, you're too late - the Dragonmen took him, I expect he's long been made a meal of,' and he laughs. Moments later, the radio crackles into life, 'Otto, this is Meyer - what are you doing? Stop, I order you!' Poking your head out of the hatch you can see the other Tiger tanks in pursuit. One of them fires - a geyser of dust erupts with a dull crump nearby. Your tank begins to climb a ridge. You reach the top when there is an explosion and a flash of flames.
