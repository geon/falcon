For a long time you struggle across the sand without being aware of moving until you enter the band of green mist. When instantly, it seems, you return to normal size and stride confidently through into a whirlpool of yellow. After a few more steps, you stop to get your bearings and doubts assail you. You realise you are hopelessly lost, the cloud closed in around you without any wind and as far as you know it could move with you so that you would never see the red moon of this world again. Though you have only yourself to blame for your predicament, you start to blame the Lords of TIME, and in particular Jobanque for not sending help for Bloodhound. Turn to 281.
