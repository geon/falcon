There is a commotion within the cloud, then it begins to flow past you until at last you are outside it and the motionless red sun glowers down on you once more. As the mist recedes at a startling rate you catch some of its thoughts. It is a Sossorus, a powerfully intelligent life form which is no more than a mist. Even in 3034 AD the Sossorri float freely in the gas cloud atmosphere of the red giant planet which is their home. You have missed the chance to learn something from this being for it is already lost to your sight. Shrugging disconsolately you walk on towards the great tower. Turn to 60.
