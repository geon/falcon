Suddenly there is a flash of light and an ear splitting explosion. The next thing you know is that you are lying sprawled in the sand. The Kublewagen is a mass of twisted metal, burning voraciously, sending up a pillar of black, oily smoke into the sky. There is no sign of Rudolf. He must have been killed, still inside the wreck of the Kublewagen. The ridge is only a few feet away. You leap up and sprint for it whilst you have the chance - the smoke from the jeep is obscuring you from the pursuing tanks and trucks. Moments later you are at its top. Turn to 396.
