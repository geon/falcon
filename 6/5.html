The Sossorros floods your mind with images of its own world. Thousands like it float free in the atmosphere of a great red gas giant of a world in the freezing atmosphere, where the gravity is still so heavy that they are smaller in volume than the one which stretches for fifty metres to either side of you. The Cloud-Being tells you that a superior life form has used an instrument of planetary travel to trap beings from many worlds and times on this world which teeters on the brink of annihilation, at the dusk of time. Suddenly the mist begins to recede away from you at a great rate. Either it is bored by your questioning or something has frightened it. Turn to 60.
