The lift door hums shut behind you and you are bathed in the glow of lights of every conceivable colour. The entire walls of the lift capsule are covered in lighted buttons - the lift must have thousands upon thousands of possible destinations. Suddenly all go dark save one flashing white light and the lift whooshes into motion. For a time you can hear the steady boom-boom of the piston, but the lift twists and turns through many shafts before coming to rest at least a mile up the tower. Turn to 224.
