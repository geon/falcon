The tower was built, but life as they are used to it is becoming impossible to sustain. Already hundreds of them have 'gone green', for they are running out of the drugs which some need to prevent an unpleasant mutation that damages the intellect. When this happens people turn a sickly shade of pale green and become scavengers.
