The unreflecting black monolith denies all of your attempts to destroy it. Bloodhound's blaster makes no impression. In the end you are forced to scour the planet for explosives. When you reach the rim of the crater again, the black monolith slides gently out of sight below the dust, as if it knew your purpose. The dust ocean reaches five hundred feet to the floor of the crater and you know you can never reach the monolith. Falcon's Wing cannot jump back. You are doomed to a twilight life at the end of time.
