The lance pennon flutters in the dust and as you lay your hands on the haft, a voice seems to speak in your mind: 'When the Infidel is driven forth from the Holy Land, then may ye attain the Grail.' The words seem meaningless to you, the lance is unwieldy, too cumbersome to carry, it carries no clues as to what is happening so you leave it buried in the dust and run back to the hillock.
