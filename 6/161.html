Just as you are overflying what looks like camouflage nets covering quite a large area your eye is caught by a flashing light on the radar display. There is a shrieking roar and a jet-propelled fighter aircraft of the early twenty-first century hurtles across your nose at mach three. It carries the letters USAF, and is armed with missiles. The fighter pulls into a steep climb and you realise that it is going to loop the loop and fly at you, from behind, perhaps on an attack run. Will you:
