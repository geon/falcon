The gravel gives way to a featureless dusty plain once more and you make towards a low pock-marked hillock. After a while you cross a set of tracks which stretch towards nothing to either side as far as the eye can see. They remind you of fish bones, a series of parallel indentations made by a very heavy vehicle, but there is no indication as to what drove it or whether it is a robot. You trudge on towards the hillock and realise that you are growing thirsty. Turn to 183.
