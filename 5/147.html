When the two ships have docked above the orange, gas-wreathed planet Barbarossa, Agents Chameleon and Blade meet you on the bridge. Chameleon is in the form of a handsome Earther in a green costume with blue circles, while the heavy Rigellian is dressed in golden battledress. You confer together and come up with a plan. Chameleon will change his appearance to look like a member of the criminal underworld of Barbarossa, Blade will not look out of place on Barbarossa which is a real melting pot of races and types. Together you can penetrate a public gathering place to search for information as to where, near the starport, Yelov's Time Machine is hidden. You decide to make disabling the machine your top priority. The area surrounding the starport is called Smugglers Port and it contains many warehouses in which a Time Machine could be easily hidden. Your own Time Machine, Falcon's Wing, has been freighted to Barbarossa under Chameleon's directive and will be kept on the Siriun ship under armed guard. Sparrowhawk and Yelov arrived on Barbarossa some hours earlier but the TIME beacon has not registered any disturbance of the time flux so Yelov's machine is still nearby. Turn to 323.
