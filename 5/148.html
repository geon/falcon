You were taken by surprise and you are too slow. Before you can act, a sticky strand of silvery thread strikes you full in the chest. Desperately you try to pull it off but your actions only serve to enmesh your hands and arms in the thread. You begin to panic. Suddenly there is a massive yank and you are pulled down through the hole and left to fall a thousand feet to the deck below. You are smeared like jam across the engines of the Ark.
