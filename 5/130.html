Falcon's Wing rematerialises in its bay at the Eiger Vault. You step out and catch the hovrail to the centre of Alpolis and your office in the TIME building. From here you will have to take a lift to one of the lower levels and take a city hovrail car to the TIME building. Much to your disgust you find that the lift has just broken down.
