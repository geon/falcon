You gun the Silver Dart forward towards the nearest lightsail and looking left and below you see another ship converging on the same lightsail at the same speed. It is the blue and white racer of the reigning champion, Lord Pilota of the TIME Executive. As you catch up the floating lightsail you are momentarily enraptured by its fragile magnificence, a great arcing span of silver, looking as if it must buckle and fall apart at any moment. Pilota's craft edges closer to your own.
