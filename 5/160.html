A bolt of white lightning streaks from your pistol, shadows flicker and flare as the Engineering Deck is lit by the flash. It shears through the thread, like a hot knife through fungspread, the tendrils shrivelling apart in the heat. There is a bubbling shriek and the Thaccoid begins to fall back into the vast emptiness below. It launches another stream of web-like thread, but it appears to have used its web reserve already, for it doesn't reach the floor of B Deck. A second or two later and you can near the awful squelching splat as it hits the ground. That is one Federation Termination Order that has been fulfilled. Collecting your wits, you turn to the steel door at the end of the Cryogenic corridor and open it. Turn to 242.
