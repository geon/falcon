There is a loud chorus of booing in the banquet hall but the jester can see that you will not be persuaded and he bows to you, a look of malicious cunning in his eyes, before cartwheeling off the stage. As a troupe of tumblers begin to leap and gambol on the stage you walk back towards the door. As you go you are relted with eggs and rotten fruit, so you hasten outside where you quickly clean yourself up a little.
