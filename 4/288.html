Although taking off the helmet has lessened the strength with which the alien will channels into your mind your worry at being lost in time has left you mentally weakened and you succumb. Your will is no longer your own and you are forced to let go of the flyer's controls. Its nose tilts gently downwards and you plough into the flowers at several times the speed of sound. The flyer bounces, hits the ground again and begins to fall apart, Seconds later the crash-cell, the cockpit itself, is rolling end over end through the flowers, cutting a swathe through them.
