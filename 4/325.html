After an hour and a half's work you have a rope knotted at intervals long enough to reach to the ground. As you climb down a giant insect which resembles a huge dandelion seed-ball with six jointed legs but without any discernible head floats above you to tree top level. At last you are down and the puff-ball being is drifting away on the breeze. The lush blue grass towers above your head, but you decide to strike out towards a huge pink flower which you glimpsed from above. The fronds of grass part easily before you, but you cannot see far ahead. Suddenly something is scurrying towards you. Its snake-like head parts the grass as it butts you so hard in the midriff that you are sent flying backwards. Lose 2 Endurance points. It is a thirty-foot cross between a reptile and a snake, its sinuous body carried along by eight squat legs. It is already enfolding you in its grey coils, its pink tongue darting out at you. Your blaster is squeezed against your side and you cannot reach it, as your suit is gradually compressed. Will you:

Use your Power of Will to attempt to control it? Turn to 343
