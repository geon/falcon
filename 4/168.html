You have only enough fuel to make one last jump, but at least it will be to Earth, even if it is in the past and there is a slight chance you will be rescued by one of TIME's agents. Worried in case Falcon's Wing is wrecked by whatever lurks outside you make what you hope will not turn out to be a rash decision and type in the co-ordinates of the timehole in Earth's past. Everything goes grey as you wink out into null-space and you spend the hour of waiting in the Autodoc, relaxing with the aid of some narcotics. Note that you have used another polybdenum rod. After an hour the ship rematerialises and the camera, free of squid-like beings now, scans the new landscape. Turn to 143.
