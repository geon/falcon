You point to your mouth and gesture as if unable to speak and, to your consternation, one of them says that he will try to cure you. You allow him to place his hands on your throat and he begins to chant words which, for some reason, your machine will not translate. When he has finished, a strange tingling overcomes you and you can feel the tonsils which were taken out when you were young regrowing. This man has remarkable powers of magical healing, if you were suffering from an irritation of the skin it is cured and you may restore your modifiers by 1 point on your Agent Profile. You pretend, however, that you are still dumb and they put a plate of food in front of you before turning to watch the antics of some scantily clad tumblers. Turn to 416.
