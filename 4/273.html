As you skim towards the huge mounds of debris which have obviously been mined out of tunnels you see several large lizards each with six splayed legs like pond-crawling spiders. They tread very carefully, as if trying not to disturb anything lurking below the ground. As you approach the nearest red slag-heap, you catch sight of a gang of the fearsome looking Repnids digging a trench.
