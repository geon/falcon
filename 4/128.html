As you step through the door you are surprised to enter a dimly lit banqueting hall, long and low. The long trestle tables are packed with revellers gorging themselves and sweating lightly in the warmth of a huge log fire, above which a boar turns on a spit. As your eyes get used to the gloom a man dressed like a jester vaults over the high table and beckons you, saying, 'Welcome, welcome to the Feast of Fools.'
