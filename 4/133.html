Your crash-couch swivels up into position in the flyer and the cockpit seals, but you have taken the precaution of wearing your environment suit. The hatch doors fly open and the flyer is catapulted out of Falcon's Wing. You glide, then cut in the Ion drive when you are safely away from your Time Machine which is surrounded by a hologram CAIN has caused to look like a rough mound of ice. You point the flyer towards the furthest horizon and hurtle towards the equator of the oddly-shaped planet. Your sensors indicate that there is almost no atmosphere, the pole rises above it, like a single smooth mountain. The flyer reaches Mach 6 easily, there is little wind resistance. The featureless plain of permafrost stretches for a hundred miles, then gives way to a moor covered with bright yellow moss. Beyond that the temperature rises suddenly and you are within a much thicker atmosphere which might possibly support human life. You begin to notice the occasional large flower below you, something like a sunflower, with deep red petals and a shining, glassy centre. As you hurtle on they become more numerous. Will you:
