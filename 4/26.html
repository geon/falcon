As you step onto the twisted streetmetal beneath Falcon's Wing you wonder what can have happened in the years since you began your mission. You thread your way between the debris of shattered buildings and wrecked jetcopters. A huge stratocruiser lies on its side against the ruins of the Health Executive Hospital. There are no living things anywhere in sight and you feel sick at heart as you turn up an arcing streetmetal span that reaches towards the SocWork building. Suddenly there is a loud whirring ahead of you and a hideous death-machine flies into sight. Ruby red lasers pulse at intervals from revolving turrets at each corner of the hovering craft.
