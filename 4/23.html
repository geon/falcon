As you concentrate your mind against their leader, the man in the rainbow robe stops gesturing and speaks the word 'Bow'. To your consternation, you find yourself bent over, looking at your feet. He is a true magician and his magic has forced you into a helpless position. The cavalry men begin to laugh uproariously at you and three of them bury their swords in your back. No one will ever know that you were the first Earther to travel into the future.
