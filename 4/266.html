You heave yourself off the spike and the plasnet seal of your suit closes the hole at once, but a terrible itching distracts your thoughts. The irritant contained in the liquid is affecting your nervous system. If this is the first time the irritant has attacked your nervous system remove 1 from ALL your modifiers until you can find an antidote. The silver blades of its mouth scythe at you as you disappear into Falcon's Wing. Surveying the insect through the camera you see that it is joined by another and then a third, even larger, insect with a spiked, coppery-black carapace and larger mandibles. You decide to engage the Variac Drive at random to leave this planet as soon as possible. Turn to 300.
