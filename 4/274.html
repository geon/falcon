As Jahkela and the land fish float serenely over the land, you bounce uncomfortably in their wake. The lower gravity means that you bound at every stride, but it is difficult to find a rhythm and you jerk along like a demented toy. The uncomfortable pursuit lasts for half an hour, by which time you are almost exhausted, but at least you do not sweat inside your suit. In the distance you can see a huge monolith, like an enormous second millennium church spire which has been set down in the midst of the empty sands. As you get closer you see that it appears to be a single crystal reaching eighty metres towards the spore-filled sky, and an archway has been hollowed in its side. Jahkela motions you imperiously to step past her through the archway. Turn to 75.
