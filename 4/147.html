As soon as you point your blaster at one of the bizarre and beautiful plants it seems to vanish. After three attempts you blast anyway, in case they are putting an illusion into your mind, but the ray seems to have no effect. It becomes uncomfortably hot inside your suit and still you cannot hit them. They are moving, always behind you, by thought alone, able to read your thoughts so that you cannot surprise them. The flowers use the crystal lens in the centre of their petals to focus the sun's rays on you. There is nothing you can do, the back of your helmet melts and your brain is cooked.
